# Claim: ModelShield detects all 10 loading-path obfuscation variants, covering
Joblib raw pickle, five Joblib compression variants (gzip, bz2, xz, lzma,
zlib), PyTorch ZIP and TAR containers, NumPy pickle-backed loading, and
one EOP bypass.

## Full Run (~3 minutes, 10 models)

    chmod +x run.sh
    sudo ./run.sh

## Verification

View the summary:

    cat ~/pickle_paths_results/summary.json

List all verdicts:

    grep "Verdict:" ~/pickle_paths_results/pickle_paths_log.txt

Count TPs and FNs:

    echo "TP: $(ls ~/pickle_paths_results/TP/ | wc -l)"
    echo "FN: $(ls ~/pickle_paths_results/FN/ | wc -l)"

## Expected Output

10 files scanned, 10 MALICIOUS, 0 BENIGN, 0 errors.
