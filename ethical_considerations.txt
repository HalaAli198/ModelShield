Ethical Considerations

1. Controlled Execution Environment

   All analysis was performed in isolated virtual machines containing
   no real user data, credentials, or sensitive information. 

2. Responsible Disclosure

   All confirmed real-world malware was reported to the relevant
   platform security teams (Hugging Face Security, AWS Trust & Safety)
   prior to publication. Detailed disclosure timelines are documented
   in each security advisory under Security_Advisories(Real_Malware_Cases)/
   in the repository root.

3. Malicious Model Handling

   The 41 malicious models are not included in the public release
   because they contain live payloads (reverse shells, credential
   exfiltration, reconnaissance scripts). The crafted evasion variants,
   TF abuse attacks, and PickleBall dataset are included as they are
   based on previously published work (Liu et al. [14], Zhu et al. [15],
   Kellas et al. [17]).
