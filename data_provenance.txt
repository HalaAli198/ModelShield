Data Provenance

1. Hugging Face, ModelScope, and Kaggle Scan (March 25 - May 5, 2026)

   145,000 models scanned via public APIs:
   - 105,000 from Hugging Face
   - 30,000 from ModelScope
   - 10,000 from Kaggle

   Only publicly accessible repositories were accessed. 41 malicious
   models were identified on Hugging Face. No malicious models were
   found on ModelScope or Kaggle within the evaluated window.

   Continued scanning through August 18, 2026 found 105 additional
   malicious models on Hugging Face.

2. Gadget Substitution Variants (15 models)

   Crafted by us based on evasion techniques reported by Liu et al.
   (reference [14] in the paper).

3. Loading-Path Obfuscation Variants (10 models)

   Crafted by us based on evasion techniques reported by Liu et al.
   (reference [14] in the paper).

4. TensorFlow SavedModel API Abuse Attacks (4 models)

   From Zhu et al. (reference [15] in the paper).

5. PickleBall Dataset (252 benign, 84 malicious)

   From Kellas et al. (reference [17] in the paper).
   Publicly available at: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16974644

6. Quick Test Samples (1 benign, 1 malicious)

   Crafted by us for smoke testing the pipeline.
