Claim: ModelShield detects 52 of the 84 malicious PickleBall models. The remaining 32 are not flagged because their payloads produce no observable malicious behavior at runtime (e.g. print-only payloads, Windows-only code, dead URLs, writes to non-sensitive paths).

Paper reference: PickleBall evaluation (to appear in the revised manuscript)

The PickleBall dataset is publicly available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16974644.

Reproduction: Run run.sh. xvfb-run is required because some payloads attempt to open a GUI. Compare the final summary against expected/expected.txt.

Actual runtime: ~38 minutes (84 models).
